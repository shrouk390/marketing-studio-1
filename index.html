Marketing Studio 1: The Investment Committee

BUS 1410 · Introduction to Marketing · Groups · 30 minutes · 2%

Your role

You are the strategy team at Nilo Tech. The CEO has called an emergency investment committee. She has 10 investment tokens to spend on next year's growth and wants your team's recommendation by the end of the session.

About Nilo Tech

Nilo Tech is an Egyptian consumer-tech company founded in Cairo in 2015. It sells through its website, Instagram and electronics shops in several cities. Most customers are students and young professionals aged 17 to 30.

Current mission statement: “We sell phone accessories and audio gadgets.”

Rules

  • Your reasoning must come from this sheet and your own judgement. There is no “right” answer, but there are better and weaker arguments.
  • Your instructor may call on any member of your group to defend your answers, so make sure everyone understands them.
