The Delta Wheels Council

BUS 1410 · Introduction to Marketing · Groups · about 25 minutes

Your role

You are the strategy team at Delta Wheels. The CEO has called an emergency council. She has 10 investment tokens to spend on next year's growth and wants your team's recommendation by the end of the session.

About Delta Wheels

Delta Wheels is an Egyptian mobility company founded in Mansoura in 2012. It sells online and through shops in Cairo, Giza, Alexandria and several Delta cities. Most customers are students, office workers and delivery riders aged 18 to 40 who live in crowded cities.

Current mission statement: “We build bikes, scooters and mopeds.”
