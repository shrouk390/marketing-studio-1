Your role

You are the strategy team at Pixel Pyramid Studios. The CEO has 10 investment tokens to spend on next year's growth and wants your team's plan by the end of the day.

About the company

Pixel Pyramid Studios is a video game company founded in Cairo in 2016. It sells games through app stores, its own website and a few game shops. Most players are students and young adults aged 15 to 30 who play on their phones and with friends.

Current mission statement: “We make video games.”
